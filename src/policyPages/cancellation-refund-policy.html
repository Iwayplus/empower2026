Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: 12/06/2025

At Empower Conference, we strive to provide all attendees with a seamless and valuable experience. We understand that unforeseen circumstances can arise. This policy outlines the terms for cancellations and refunds.

1. General Policy

All registrations for Empower Conference are considered final. However, cancellations may be considered on a case-by-case basis under the following conditions:

2. Valid Reasons for Cancellation

Cancellations will be permitted only in the event of the following genuine emergencies:

  • Medical emergencies (with supporting medical documents)
  • Serious personal emergencies such as bereavement or accidents
  • Other unavoidable and verifiable emergencies (subject to review)

3. Request Procedure

  • Cancellation requests must be emailed to info@empowerconference.in with subject line: “Cancellation Request – [Your Name]”.
  • You must attach valid documentation (e.g., medical certificate, travel restrictions, etc.)
  • All requests must be submitted no later than 5 days before the event start date, except in extreme emergencies.

4. Refund Terms

  • If approved, the refund amount will be processed within 14 working days.
  • A processing fee (if applicable) or transaction charges may be deducted.
  • No refunds will be issued for cancellations made without valid proof or after the event.

5. Non-Refundable Conditions

  • No-show at the event without notice or supporting documents
  • Voluntary withdrawal or dissatisfaction with event content
  • Inability to attend due to minor schedule conflicts

6. Contact
