Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the Empower App (the “App”) and visit https://empowerconference.in (the “Site”). Empower App is designed to support conference registration, participation, and indoor navigation using smartphone sensors.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information (Provided by You)

  • Name, email, phone number
  • Organization and job title
  • Session preferences or feedback

B. Device and Sensor Data (Collected Automatically)

  • Device type, OS version, app version
  • Location data (approximate indoor positioning via Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, or device sensors)
  • Motion sensor data (e.g., accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer for indoor path tracking)
  • Bluetooth and Wi-Fi state (used for indoor navigation)
  • Anonymous usage logs and crash reports

C. Usage Data

  • Pages/screens viewed, buttons clicked
  • Time spent in app, navigation behavior

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To register and manage your conference experience
  • To personalize session recommendations
  • To enable indoor navigation within the conference venue
  • To send important updates, alerts, and notifications
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To troubleshoot issues and perform analytics

3. Permissions We Request

  • Location Access: for indoor positioning
  • Bluetooth: for detecting nearby beacons or mesh devices
  • Motion Sensors: for step counting and real-time navigation
  • Internet: for content updates and syncing your data

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share anonymized or necessary data:

  • With third-party services that help us operate the app (e.g., Firebase, analytics providers)
  • With event organizers (only if required and disclosed in advance)
  • To comply with legal obligations or respond to valid legal requests

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data from unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for conference operations or as required by law. Navigation logs are stored temporarily and anonymized.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access or update your personal data
  • Withdraw consent or delete your account
  • Disable location and Bluetooth from device settings (may affect app functionality)

8. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for use by individuals under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in-app and on our website with an updated effective date.
